Kit Combat S01 S02 Act One
Session One · Look for the Light · Ashline Territory

KVLR
1490 AM

A Firefly courier named Wren gave you a frequency, a hand-drawn schematic and a promise that someone is still listening. The tower on Ludlow Ridge has been dark for eleven years. You are going to turn it back on.

Players5 · Novice Survivor
Runtime2.5 hours
RulesStandard — no Trauma, no Grounded
Travel modeLinear
ObjectivePower the transmitter, make contact

Cold open — read this, then ask the question

The padlock closes with a sound like a dropped wrench, and for exactly four seconds you let yourselves believe that was the hard part.

Behind you, on the far side of the chain-link, the ones that followed you up the ridge road are already pressing their faces into the fence. Good. Let them.

Ahead of you is the parking lot of KVLR — forty years of dead cars sunk to their axles in weeds, a snapped radio mast lying across two of them, and a low brick building with an antenna tower rising out of it into the grey.

And in the lot, between you and that building, six shapes that were not there when you scouted this from the road. One of them lifts its head. The sound it makes is not quite a word.

You just locked yourselves in with them. Nobody is opening that gate again. What do you do?

The scenes Read-aloud, branches, DCs

01

The Lot

Set piece · forced

What it is

Seventy metres of collapsed parking lot between the gate and the building. Cars sunk in weeds give cover and sightline breaks the whole way across; the fallen mast makes a raised walkway most of the distance if anyone thinks to climb it.

Five Runners scattered and idle among the vehicles. One Clicker stands at the loading dock ramp, which is exactly where the quiet route is. It has not moved in a long time.

Why this opening works

Every player learns the three core verbs in ten minutes: sneak, grapple, run. Nobody has to be told combat is dangerous — a Runner does 3D8 against 25 Health, and they'll feel that on the first hit.

Branches out

  • LOUDThe front doors → 02. Glass, chained from inside. Brutality DC 8 to break the chain.Every Runner in the lot converges. The Ashline inside start Alert.
  • QUIETThe dock ramp → 03. The roll-up door sits a foot off the ground. Getting there means handling the Clicker: sneak past Sneak DC 10, or shiv it (auto-kill on a passed sneak).Fails here go straight to combat — Clickers skip Alert.
  • CLIMBThe fire escape → 04. Bottom ladder is retracted. Elusive DC 9, or a Boost — one PC rolls Brutality, the climber rolls Elusive, combined vs DC 12.Slow. Whoever goes last is alone in the lot for a round.
  • FINDScavenging DC 7 in the lot: a siphon hose in a truck bed, 1D10 Parts, a bolt cutter under the mast (opens the chain silently — reward the player who searches before charging).
02

The Lobby

Combat

What it is

A reception desk, a wall of dead station photographs, and a smear of grey ash drawn across the doorframe at shoulder height — deliberate, recent, and repeated on every interior door in the building.

Instinct DC 7 reads it correctly: this is a territory mark, and someone is maintaining it. That is the party's first warning that the station is occupied, not just standing.

If they came in loud

Two Ashline are already waiting behind the desk with the lobby as their cover. Give them Semi-Cover and let the party learn that cover only helps against guns.

What's here

  • Ashline Thug — 2x4, 1D8, 12 HP, Hit DC 5, Counter DC 6
  • Ashline Ruffian — bat, 1D10, 15 HP, Hit DC 6, Counter DC 6
  • Visitor lanyard drawer — Scavenging DC 7. A staff keycard: opens the second-floor stair door without noise.
  • Goods theme loot — one roll, Scavenging DC 7

Branches out

  • → 06The corridor to the bullpen. Only exit — the lobby is a dead end by design, which is the price of the loud entrance.
03

The Loading Bay

Stealth · objective item

What it is

The Ashline larder. Crates stacked by type, hand-lettered: BRASS · GLASS · MEAT · SALT. One man, alone, counting shells into a coffee tin by lamplight with his back to the door.

This is the single best stealth-kill setup in the session, and it is also the session's first moral question: he is unarmed, he is doing inventory, and he has no idea they exist.

The twist, planted here

The coffee tin is full of ammunition and the man has no gun. The Ashline trade brass — it's their currency, so they don't spend it on themselves. That is why every enemy in this building carries a bat instead of a rifle. Let a player work it out rather than telling them.

What's here

  • Ashline Crew — pistol holstered and unloaded, 18 HP, Sneak DC 6. Stealth kill: pass Sneak DC 6, then Brutality vs Hit DC 7. A shiv skips the second roll.
  • Three jerry cans — two full, one empty. The generator needs two. This is objective item 1 of 3.
  • Utility theme loot — 10–25 Parts, adhesive, blades
  • The trade ledger's duplicate — Scavenging DC 9. Names a FEDRA patrol as a trading partner.

Branches out

  • → 06Through the service corridor into the bullpen — arrives at the camp's flank.
  • → 05The rear stair up to the offices. Elusive DC 6 to climb quietly; the treads are steel.
04

The Roof

Puzzle · objective item

What it is

Gravel, standing water, and the tower going up into low cloud. The antenna feed cable has been cut about three metres up and the copper stripped out of it — recently, and not by weather.

Face-down beside the tower base is a man in a Firefly windbreaker, eleven months dead, a coil of good cable still clipped to his belt. This is the last person Wren sent. She did not mention him.

His notebook

Water-damaged, mostly frequencies. The last legible line is the transmitter room door code — 4-9-1-1 — and underneath it, in different ink: "they let me get this far." The party can now open the transmitter room without ever meeting Boone.

What's here

  • Objective item 2 of 3 — the cable coil. Maintenance DC 9 to splice the antenna feed, with adhesive and blades. Without them, DC 12.
  • The door code — bypasses Boone's key entirely
  • Sightline — the whole lot below. A player who looks can count the Runners and see the gate.
  • No enemies. Let the roof be quiet. They will need one quiet scene.

Branches out

  • → 05The roof hatch down into the second floor. Unlocked, loud hinges — Elusive DC 7 or the offices know they're coming.
  • → 08The cable run. An external conduit ladder descends the back wall straight into the transmitter loft. Elusive DC 10, exposed the whole way. Skips 05, 06 and 07 — but they arrive with no fuel and no key.
05

The Offices

Stealth · recon

What it is

Sales cubicles, a break room, a wall of station-anniversary plaques. Ash lines on three of the six doors — the Ashline haven't cleared this floor, they've simply marked what they use.

The interior window overlooking the newsroom is broken out. From here the party can watch the camp below for as long as they like — count the men, hear the conversation, learn Boone's name before they ever meet him. Reward patience with information.

What's here

  • Spool of speaker wire — an inferior substitute for the roof cable. Maintenance DC 12 instead of DC 9.
  • A working flashlight and 1D6 batteries — Scavenging DC 7
  • Crafting theme loot — one roll
  • Overheard from below — Boone arguing with his own people about whether to move the post before winter. He is losing the argument.

Branches out

  • → 06The rear stair down into the bullpen, arriving behind the camp.
  • → 07Drop on the booth. Through the broken window onto the booth roof, Elusive DC 11. Puts them on Boone alone, with his crew on the far side of a door. A decapitation strike — and it skips every conversation that would have made him a person.
06

The Bullpen

Converge · three modes

What it is

The old newsroom, cleared to the walls and turned into a supply post. Sleeping mats, a stove vented through a smashed window, crates sorted by the same four words as the loading bay. A trade ledger open on a desk, kept in a careful hand.

All three routes meet here — but they arrive differently. Loud entry means the room is Alert and facing the corridor. The dock and the stairs both arrive on a flank.

The ledger

Scavenging DC 5, or just reading it. Columns of trades: brass out, salt and meat in. Three entries are with "the patrol" — FEDRA. One entry, eleven months old, reads "firefly — nothing owed."

What's here

  • Ashline Ruffian — bat, 1D10, 15 HP, Hit DC 6
  • Ashline Robber — upgraded pipe, 2D10, 25 HP, Hit DC 5, Counter DC 8
  • Full cover everywhere — desks, filing cabinets, a toppled vending machine

Three ways through

  • FIGHTTwo Ashline plus Boone joining on round 2 from the booth. With five PCs this is winnable but costly — expect one survivor at 0 HP.
  • SNEAKGroup Elusive vs Sneak DC 6 to cross to the booth door. Advantage if they killed the loading bay man quietly.
  • TALKAnyone who calls out rather than shooting gets Boone, unarmed, out of the booth. Straight to 07 with the crew watching.
07

The Booth

Social · Boone

What it is

The soundproofed broadcast booth, glass mostly intact, foam wedges still on the walls. Boone sleeps in here because it's the only room in the building where you can't hear anything coming — which tells you what he's afraid of.

Boone is not a psychopath. He's a foreman running a supply post for about twenty people who live two ridges over, and he has a real objection: a lit tower brings traffic, and traffic brings the patrol he's been carefully feeding just enough to be left alone.

Play him tired, not menacing

He asks what's on the other end of the signal. If a player says "the Fireflies", he laughs — not cruelly. He says: "Eleven months ago a kid in a yellow jacket said the same thing. He went up on my roof and never came down. I didn't kill him. The roof did." Whether that's true is up to how the party has behaved.

What he has

  • The transmitter key — on a lanyard, objective item 3 of 3 (or bypass with the roof code)
  • Statline if it comes to it — machete 2D20, 33 HP, Hit DC 7, Sneak DC 6, Dodge DC 8, Counter DC 9. He is by far the most dangerous thing in the building.

What he'll take

  • DEALA broadcast window. The tower runs only between set hours, and the Ashline get told the schedule. Costs nothing, changes everything later.
  • DEALTrade. All the party's ammunition, or a working gun, for the key and safe passage out through the bolt-hole.
  • KILLFast and expensive. His crew fight to the last after that, and the bolt-hole exit is lost.
  • BYPASSThey already have the door code from the roof. They can walk past this entire scene — and should be allowed to, if that's what they earned.
08

The Transmitter Room

Puzzle

What it is

A hot little concrete room full of racked equipment, one steel door with a keypad, and a diesel generator that has not turned over since 2013. Three things must be true before the tower lights.

  • 1 · Fuel — two full jerry cans from the loading bay (03)
  • 2 · Antenna feed — spliced cable from the roof (04), Maintenance DC 9
  • 3 · Access — Boone's key (07), the roof code (04), or Brutality DC 11 to force the door — loud, and it brings the bullpen

Getting it running

Maintenance DC 10 to repair the generator's seized starter. On a success it coughs, catches, and the room fills with noise and diesel smoke.

On a natural 1 the generator is permanently dead — the handbook is explicit about this. Do not soften it. Instead reveal the fallback: a rack of station backup batteries, holding just enough charge for one four-minute broadcast, and no second chance. This is a better scene than the success was.

Pacing note

If you're past the two-hour mark when they get here, hand them the repair on a straight DC 10 and go straight to 09. The climax needs its twenty minutes more than the puzzle needs its third roll.

09

The Broadcast

Set piece · 4 rounds

What it is

The ON AIR lamp comes on. It is the first electric light any of these people have seen in years, and for a second nobody moves.

Then the tower starts to hum — and outside, in the lot, every Runner they slipped past turns toward the building at once.

Four rounds. Someone has to sit at the desk and hold the transmit key while the rest hold the corridor. Runners arrive on rounds 2, 3 and 4 — two, then two, then the Clicker if it's still alive.

What comes back

Round 2 — static, then a whoop of feedback.

Round 3 — Wren's voice, three ridges away, badly compressed and openly crying: "KVLR. KVLR. Oh, you beautiful idiots."

Round 4 — Wren cuts out. A second voice comes in on the same frequency. Older. Unhurried. Not surprised.

End the session here

"KVLR, KVLR. This is Sundial. We have been sitting on this frequency for six years waiting for somebody to be stupid enough to answer. Do not move. We are closer than you think."

Then stop. Do not explain who Sundial is — not this session, possibly not this year.

10

The Way Out

Chase · if time allows

What it is

The gate is still locked, and the crowd is between them and it. This is a five-minute pressure scene, not a fight — narrate it fast and let them spend whatever they've got.

If you're out of time, skip it: end on Sundial's voice and open next session with the run. That's a stronger cut anyway.

Exits, best to worst

  • ALLIEDBoone's bolt-hole. A cut section of fence behind the dumpsters, only if they dealt with him. Free and clean.
  • CLIMBThe roof plank. Elusive DC 9 to cross to the adjacent maintenance shed. Costs the plank.
  • FIGHTBack to the gate. Straight through whatever's left. Survival DC 8 to pick the line; failure means one PC is cut off alone.

Before Friday Prep checklist

  • Print five character sheets plus the Survivor's Journal page
  • Decide the region. Ludlow Ridge can be anywhere — relocating it near your own town pays off enormously with a table of locals
  • Index-card the ash line so you remember to describe it on every interior door
  • Write Boone's first line down and read it, don't improvise it
  • Pre-roll the Runners' HP (15–25 each) so you're not rolling mid-scene
  • Print the door code 4-9-1-1 on a scrap of paper to hand over physically
  • Have the combat card open — Dodge DCs and the Overwhelming Odds rule are what you'll reach for
  • Decide now whether Boone is telling the truth about the dead Firefly. You will be asked.
  • Session Zero first — 45 minutes, everyone builds together. Push for one strong Maintenance and one Medicine.
  • Do not name Sundial. Not this session.
